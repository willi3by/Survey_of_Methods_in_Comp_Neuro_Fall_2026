# Week 7 data dictionary

The dataset is synthetic and designed for teaching neural time-series analysis.

## lfp_trials.csv

Each row is one time sample from one trial.

- `animal_id`: animal identifier
- `trial_id`: trial number within animal
- `time_s`: time in seconds from the start of the trial
- `hippocampus_uV`: simulated hippocampal LFP-like voltage in microvolts
- `pfc_uV`: simulated prefrontal-cortex LFP-like voltage in microvolts

## animal_metadata.csv

One row per animal.

- `animal_id`: animal identifier
- `treatment`: Control or Stimulated
- `treatment_binary`: 0 for Control, 1 for Stimulated
- `sampling_rate_hz`: sampling frequency in Hz

There are 12 animals, 8 trials per animal, 5 seconds per trial, sampled at 200 Hz.
